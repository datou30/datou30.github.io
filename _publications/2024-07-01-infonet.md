Developed a pretrained tabular model for mutual information estimation between random variables, achieving 1,000× faster estimation and higher accuracy on low-dimensional benchmarks. Downstream applications include feature selection, image segmentation and keyframe extraction.

<i>Authors:</i> Zhengyang Hu, Song Kang, Qunsong Zeng, Kaibin Huang, Yanchao Yang.
