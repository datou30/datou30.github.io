Invited departmental seminar at HKU ECE (20 May 2026, 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM).

The talk covered **InfoNet** (ICML 2024, Oral) and **InfoAtlas** (ICML 2026), focusing on pretrained tabular models for statistical dependence estimation. It also discussed online diagnostics based on statistical dependence among hidden representations to identify Transformer training anomalies, including overfitting, data anomalies and loss spikes, with validation on NanoGPT, ViT and Pythia.
