Oral presentation at **ICML 2024** for our paper *InfoNet: Neural Estimation of Mutual Information without Test-Time Optimization*.

The talk introduced InfoNet, a pretrained tabular model for dependence estimation between random variables, achieving **1,000× faster estimation** and higher accuracy on low-dimensional benchmarks. Downstream applications include feature selection, image segmentation and keyframe extraction.
